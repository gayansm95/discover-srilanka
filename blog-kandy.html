Kandy – The Sacred City

Kandy is the cultural heart of Sri Lanka, home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. Surrounded by lush hills and a serene lake, the city offers a unique blend of history, spirituality, and natural beauty.

Highlights include the Royal Botanical Gardens, the Kandy Lake, and the annual Esala Perahera festival. Explore vibrant markets selling traditional crafts and taste authentic Kandyan cuisine.

Sigiriya – The Lion Rock

Rising dramatically from the plains, Sigiriya is an ancient rock fortress and UNESCO World Heritage site. Climb to the summit to see the remnants of a 5th-century palace and breathtaking frescoes.

The water gardens, boulder gardens, and the Mirror Wall are must‑see features. The panoramic views from the top are unforgettable.

Ella – Hill Country Escape

Ella is a charming hill town famous for its tea plantations, hiking trails, and the iconic Nine Arch Bridge. The cool climate and misty mountains create a relaxing atmosphere.

Popular activities include the Ella Rock hike, Little Adam’s Peak, and visiting the Demodara railway loop. Enjoy a train ride through the tea fields.

Yala National Park – Wildlife Safari

Yala is Sri Lanka’s most famous wildlife destination, renowned for its leopard population. A safari through the park offers encounters with elephants, sloth bears, and a variety of birds.

Morning and evening game drives provide the best opportunities to spot these magnificent creatures in their natural habitat.

Galle – Colonial Heritage

The Galle Fort, a UNESCO site, is a living colonial town with cobblestone streets, Dutch buildings, and charming boutiques. It overlooks the Indian Ocean and is a blend of European and Asian cultures.

Visit the lighthouse, the Dutch Reformed Church, and enjoy fresh seafood at the fort’s restaurants. Nearby Unawatuna beach is perfect for a swim.

Cultural Triangle – Ancient Wonders

The Cultural Triangle of Sri Lanka connects Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, and Sigiriya. This region is packed with UNESCO World Heritage Sites, ancient stupas, and royal palaces.

Explore the ancient cities, see the sacred Bo Tree, and climb the rock fortress of Sigiriya for a journey back in time.

Nuwara Eliya – Little England

Nuwara Eliya is a cool hill station with manicured gardens, colonial bungalows, and world‑renowned tea estates. It’s known as “Little England” for its British‑era charm.

Highlights include Gregory Lake, the Hakgala Botanical Gardens, and a visit to a tea factory to see how Ceylon tea is made.

Eastern Sri Lanka – Beaches & Beyond

The east coast of Sri Lanka boasts pristine beaches like Trincomalee and Pasikudah, perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and whale watching.

Explore the historic Koneswaram Temple, relax on Nilaveli beach, and enjoy fresh seafood by the ocean.

Down South – Coastal Bliss

The southern coast of Sri Lanka is famous for its golden beaches, whale watching, and the historic Galle Fort. Mirissa and Unawatuna offer perfect beach holidays.

Enjoy surfing, diving, and sunset strolls along the palm-fringed shores.

Sri Lanka Safaris – Wild Encounters

Sri Lanka is a wildlife lover's paradise. From Yala's leopards to Udawalawe's elephants and Wilpattu's birdlife, each national park offers a unique safari experience.

Embark on a jeep safari with an expert naturalist and witness the stunning biodiversity of the island.

Northern Sri Lanka – Off the Beaten Path

Jaffna and the northern peninsula offer a distinct Tamil culture, ancient temples, and untouched islands. It's a region rich in history and tradition.

Visit the Nallur Kandaswamy Temple, explore the Jaffna Fort, and taste the famous Jaffna crab curry.

Arugam Bay – Surfer's Paradise

Located on the east coast, Arugam Bay is a world‑class surfing destination. The laid‑back vibe, golden sands, and consistent waves attract surfers from around the globe.

Besides surfing, explore the nearby Kumana National Park, lagoons, and enjoy fresh seafood at beachside cafés.
