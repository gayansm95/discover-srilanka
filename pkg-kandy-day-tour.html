Kandy Day Tour Experience

Explore the cultural heart of Sri Lanka – a UNESCO World Heritage City filled with ancient temples, royal gardens, and breathtaking views in just one unforgettable day.

Eight Hours Around Kandy – Day Tour

UNESCO World Heritage City

Sri Dalada Maligawa – Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Kandy, a UNESCO World Heritage City, is the cultural heart of Sri Lanka. At the center of its charm stands the Sri Dalada Maligawa, the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic of Lord Buddha. Renowned for its magnificent architecture, ornate decorative walls, golden roof, intricate woodwork, and fascinating museums, the temple offers a deeply spiritual experience.
